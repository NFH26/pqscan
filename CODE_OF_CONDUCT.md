# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people and assume they are being straight with you. Disagree about the
technical substance as sharply as you like; do not make it about the person.

## Expected

- Engage with what someone actually said or wrote.
- Say when you are unsure, and say when you were wrong. This project has a running record of
  bugs found by people who took a second look, and that record is an asset.
- Accept that a rule reading can be contested. Bring the ISM wording and argue the reading.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information, or
sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/nayefalharbi/pqscan/security/advisories/new),
which is private, or by an issue if the matter is not sensitive. Reports are handled discreetly.
The maintainer may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that are not consistent
with this document, and may ban a contributor for sustained or serious breaches.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.
